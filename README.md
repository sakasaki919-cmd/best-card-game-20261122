## The game

A turn-based card duel against the rival keeper Brine. Both sides have 30 life and a growing ember pool; each turn you play cards from a 5-card hand (click, or keys 1–5; Enter ends the turn). Four original suits — Flame, Leaf, Stone, Tide, told apart by colour *and* shape — form an advantage cycle (Flame>Leaf>Stone>Tide>Flame, +2 damage). 24 original cards in four kinds: strikes, heals (mend), wards, and card-draw (surge), each with a readable cost gem and power badge. The rival AI picks advantage strikes, heals when hurt, wards when threatened, and draws when thin. First to 0 life loses.

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser and play — no network needed. Sounds are synthesized WebAudio; all art is canvas-drawn.
